# Code of Conduct

KnewStudio contributors are expected to make participation respectful, welcoming, and free from harassment.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, accept constructive feedback, and avoid discriminatory, sexualized, threatening, or abusive language or conduct. Do not publish another person's private information or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the project or its community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's available private contact channel. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
